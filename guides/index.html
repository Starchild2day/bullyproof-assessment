The Bullyproof Quick Help Guides

The exact words for the hardest moments: what to say, what not to say, and why it matters. Twenty-five situations, each one short enough to read in the driveway.

For example:

You have full access to every guide on this device.

You didn't get training for this.

  • Nobody sat you down and showed you what to say when your child comes home crying and won't tell you why.
  • Nobody taught you the sentence to use when they say nobody likes me and mean it.
  • Nobody handed you the script for the moment your child tells you something and then asks you not to tell anyone.

You're expected to handle it anyway.

In the moment. With your own history running in the background and your own fear making noise and no rehearsal whatsoever.

Most parenting advice assumes you have time.

  • Time to read the book.
  • Time to think it through.
  • Time to cool down and come back to it tomorrow with a clear head.

You don't have time.

You have right now, in the driveway, with your child in the passenger seat.

That's what these guides are for.

  1. One situationEach one covers a single situation.
  2. The exact wordsEach one gives you the exact words — what to say, what not to say, and why the difference matters.
  3. Fast enoughEach one is short enough to read in the time it takes to park the car.
  4. A deeper layerAnd each one has a longer layer underneath, for when you want to understand what's actually happening and not just what to say about it.

You will not find every answer here. Some situations need a teacher, a counselor, or a professional. When that's true, the guide will tell you so plainly.

But for the twenty-five situations parents most often find themselves in — the ones where you're on your own and the moment is now — this is the whole set.
