Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Parents trust us with some of the hardest moments in their family's life. This policy explains, in plain words, what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Bullyproof.Guide helps parents and caregivers respond to bullying and build confident, connected kids. This policy covers Bullyproof.Guide, the Parent Clarity Check (our free assessment), the action plan emails it sends, and the Bullyproof Parent Playbook reservation list.

What we collect

  • Your answers. When you take the Parent Clarity Check, we collect your answers, such as your child's age range, what's happening, and anything you choose to type in your own words.
  • Your email address. We need it to send your action plan.
  • Your Playbook reservation. If you choose to reserve a copy of the Bullyproof Parent Playbook, we keep your email address and your permission to contact you.
  • Basic technical information. Like nearly every website, our hosting and content providers automatically receive information such as your IP address and browser type when pages load.
We never ask for your child's name, and we ask that you don't include names or other identifying details in your answers. If you have more than one child, simply call them Child A and Child B.

The assessment also saves a small note in your own browser (not on our servers) so it can remember that you recently finished a plan. You can clear it anytime by clearing your browser's site data.

How we use it

  • To build your personalized action plan and send it to you.
  • To keep children safe. If your answers suggest someone may be at risk of harm, your plan shows crisis resources first, and we are notified so we can review it. We are not an emergency service and do not monitor answers in real time. If anyone is in immediate danger, call 911. For a mental health crisis, call or text 988.
  • To send you the Playbook invitation and occasional updates, but only if you said yes.
  • To improve the assessment by looking at overall patterns, never to single out a family.

How we share it

We never sell or rent your information, and we never share it with others for their advertising. We use a small number of trusted service providers who process information only to run our services for us:

  • Netlify hosts our website and securely stores assessment submissions and reservations.
  • Resend delivers our emails.
  • Google Fonts and Cloudflare's cdnjs provide the lettering and the PDF tool our pages load.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or if we believe in good faith that it's needed to protect someone's safety.

Children's privacy

Our services are made for parents and caregivers who are 18 or older. They are not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Links to other sites

Our plans link to other websites, such as Amazon for recommended books, Bullyproof.Support, and directories of licensed professionals. Those sites have their own privacy policies. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases; Amazon may use cookies to know that you arrived from our link.

Emails and your choices

  • Your action plan email is a one-time message you asked for.
  • We send the Playbook invitation and updates only if you reserved a copy. To stop them, reply "unsubscribe" to any email or write to mark@bullyproof.guide. We honor requests within 10 business days.
  • Every email includes our mailing address and a way to reach us.

Deleting your information

You can ask us to delete your answers and email address at any time. Use the "Delete my data" link in your email, or write to mark@bullyproof.guide. We will delete your information from our records and from our service providers' systems within 30 days and let you know when it's done. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to see or correct the information we hold about you. Just ask.

How long we keep it

We keep assessment submissions only as long as we need them to deliver and review your plan and improve the assessment. We keep Playbook reservations until the Playbook launches or until you unsubscribe, whichever comes first, unless you join and choose to stay in touch.

How we protect it

All pages and forms use encrypted (HTTPS) connections, and we limit access to the information we hold. No method of storing or sending information online is perfectly secure, but we work hard to protect yours.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we'll make it clear on the site before it takes effect.

Questions about privacy? We're happy to help.
